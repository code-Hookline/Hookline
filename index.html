How it works

Three steps. No submit button.

1

Calibrate your pace once

Read a short paragraph out loud. Hookline measures your real words per minute for three delivery styles — conversational, energetic, slow.

2

Write in blocks

Stack typed blocks and write the words. The timeline re-times every block live against your calibrated pace as you type — no generic averages.

3

Export and shoot

Teleprompter PDF, director's brief, shot list — or batch-ZIP a whole campaign. Private notes are stripped from every export.

Why creators stay

It times to your real voice

A 40-second script reads back as 0:40 at your pace — not at a flat 130 wpm someone averaged. That is the instant the tool stops feeling generic and starts feeling built for you. Most teleprompters guess. Hookline measured you.

Your swipe file compounds

Rate your best hooks, save the frameworks that win in your niche. After 40 saved hooks the blank page is gone for good.

Batch your filming day

Write a week of videos, batch-export every teleprompter as one ZIP, and shoot them all in a single sitting — not one at a time.

What we deliberately leave out

No AI script generation

A thinking tool, not a content farm. You write the words; Hookline structures and times them.

No video editing

Pre-production only. It ends when you walk in front of the camera and feeds whatever editor comes next.

No cloud, no account

IndexedDB is the database; the device is the server. Your scripts never live on someone else's machine.

No platform integrations

No pulls from TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube. We track creative patterns — never platform metrics.

The next time you reshoot

You will write the script anyway — in a notes app, eyeballing the length, then reshooting because it ran fifteen seconds long. Write it once here, timed to your voice, and walk to the camera with the teleprompter already done.
